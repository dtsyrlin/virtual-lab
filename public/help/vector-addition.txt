On the left coordinate plane, drag the tip of the blue factory vector to set its length and direction (up to 10 cm). Its horizontal and vertical components update automatically.

Click the > button to create a copy of the vector (blue) and its x (orange) and y (green) components in the workspace on the right. Repeat with different factory settings to create more vectors.

Drag any created vector or component by its arrow or tail to reposition it in the workspace. Bring an arrowhead near another vector’s tail to snap them together.

Click Add to draw a purple resultant for each connected chain of two or more vectors. You can drag the purple resultant independently for comparison.

Click Clear to remove workspace vectors, components, and resultants. The factory vector remains available.

Drag the ruler or protractor to position it for measurements. Rotate the ruler by dragging one of its ends; rotate the protractor using its rotation handles.
