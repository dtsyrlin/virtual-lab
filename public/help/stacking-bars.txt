Use the Bar A, Bar B, and Target controls to choose lengths. Drag bars into the workspace and arrange them end-to-end or stack them to compare lengths. Nearby bar ends and edges snap into alignment.

With Unlimited Supply enabled, dragging from Bar A or Bar B creates another copy. Turn it off to move the original bar instead.

Select R1 or R2 on a length control to generate a new random length within the displayed range. Switching between R1 and R2 generates another random length and hides its numerical value.

The Target bar cannot be dragged. You can use the ruler to measure lengths. Changing a length control clears the bars previously copied into the workspace.

Rotate the ruler by dragging one of its ends.
