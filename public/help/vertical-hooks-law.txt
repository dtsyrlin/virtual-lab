Drag a spring from one of the spring factories to the top support and release it near the attachment point. The first object must be a spring.

To extend the hanging chain, drag another spring or a weight to the free bottom end of the last attached object. Release it close to the connection point to snap it into place.

Drag an attached weight up or down with the left mouse button, then release it to set the system moving.

To disconnect an attached spring or weight, hold the right mouse button and drag it away. Disconnect from the free end of the chain first.

To slow the oscillations, move the mouse pointer over a weight and hold it there.

Use the ruler and timer to measure length and elapsed time.
