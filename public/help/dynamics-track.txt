To change the incline, drag the right end of the track upward or downward. The left end stays in place. The track can tilt up to 15°.

Select a mass (1–5 kg) using the Mass control, then drag a block from the factory at the upper left onto the track. Repeat to add more blocks.

Drag a block along the track to reposition it. Give it a quick push by dragging and releasing it while moving to launch it with an initial velocity.

Use the Static friction coefficient and Kinetic friction coefficient controls to change the friction between the blocks and track.

Choose Elastic or Inelastic to change how blocks collide. Use Reset to remove the blocks and clear measurements.

Drag the two photogates (G1 and G2) along the track to position them. Each gate measures how long a block interrupts its beam; the Gate-to-gate display measures the time between the two gates.

Use the ruler to measure distances. Rotate the ruler by dragging one of its ends.
