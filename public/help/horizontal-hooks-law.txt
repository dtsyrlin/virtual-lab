Drag a spring from one of the spring factories to the left support and release it near the attachment point. The first object must be a spring.

To extend the chain, drag another spring or a weight to the free right end of the last attached object. Release it close to the connection point to snap it into place.

To connect the movable right support, drag it so its attachment point meets the free end of a spring, then release. The chain must end with a spring to make this connection.

Once connected, drag the right support left or right to stretch or compress the springs.

Drag an attached weight left or right with the left mouse button and release it to set the system moving.

To disconnect an attached spring, weight, or the right support, hold the right mouse button and drag it away. Disconnect from the free end first.

To slow the oscillations, move the mouse pointer over a weight and hold it there.

Use the ruler and timer to measure length and elapsed time.
